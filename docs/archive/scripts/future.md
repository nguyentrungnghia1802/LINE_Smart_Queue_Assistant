# Future Product Ideas

These ideas are not implemented or committed product scope. Each requires a separate design,
security, privacy, and acceptance review.

1. **Durable operations console:** searchable LINE/email/payment delivery failures with safe replay and cancellation controls.
2. **Production Japanese payment adapters:** add KOMOJU, PayPay, or Stripe Japan behind the existing payment-provider boundary.
3. **Multi-channel tenant LINE configuration:** allow approved organizations to use their own Official Account while preserving tenant isolation.
4. **Appointment calendar:** combine scheduled appointments and walk-in queues with capacity-aware slot allocation.
5. **Customer accessibility mode:** large text, high contrast, screen-reader-optimized ticket updates, and multilingual voice prompts.
6. **Demand-aware shift planning:** convert measured staffing recommendations into draft rosters with manager approval and export.
7. **Inventory procurement alerts:** supplier lead times, reorder points, and branch transfer suggestions for finite products.
8. **Customer feedback loop:** request post-service ratings through LINE and connect aggregate feedback to branch operations.
9. **Enterprise audit export:** signed CSV/JSON exports with retention policies and organization-owner access controls.
10. **Resilience and SLO dashboard:** booking latency, worker delay, notification success, payment reconciliation, and queue availability targets.
